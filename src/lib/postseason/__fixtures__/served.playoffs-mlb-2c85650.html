How the computer picked

Each club gets a rating from its regular-season results. The computer then plays out the postseason 10,000 times from those ratings and picks the side that won each matchup more often. A pick's chance is how often it won that matchup in the simulated postseasons where that matchup came up, and its length is how many games the pick most often took to win it.

The inputs were locked on September 28, 2026, before Game 1. The bracket was computed and locked on September 30, 2026 from those locked inputs, with the rating, simulation and bracket code unchanged since the inputs were locked. Every chance and title odd on this page is as it stood when the bracket was locked. Postseason results are not among the inputs, so a pick can name a club that was already out by then.

Run on the 2025 MLB postseason with the same settings, the computer called 5 of 11 series and got the champion wrong.

Fingerprints

Each is a SHA-256 fingerprint of something that was locked: four of the inputs and the locked bracket file. The bracket format and the locked bracket file are fingerprinted as files, the other three as their locked data written out in a fixed order. A change to any of them would change its fingerprint.
