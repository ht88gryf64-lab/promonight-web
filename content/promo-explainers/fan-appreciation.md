title: What to expect on a fan appreciation night
faqs:
  - q: Is fan appreciation night only for season-ticket holders?
    a: No. Season-ticket members usually get extra recognition, but the game, giveaways and drawings are open to all fans.
  - q: When is fan appreciation night?
    a: Typically the final home game or final home stand of the regular season.
---
Fan appreciation nights usually land in the last home stand of the season. Teams typically bundle several things into one game: a giveaway at the gates, concession discounts, in-game prize drawings with seat-location winners and on-field or on-ice recognition for season-ticket members. Players sometimes hand out items or sign autographs before the game.

Because the prizes are often drawn by seat location, stay through the game if you want a shot at them. If a giveaway is part of the night, the usual gate-giveaway rules apply: arrive when gates open, one per ticket, while supplies last.
