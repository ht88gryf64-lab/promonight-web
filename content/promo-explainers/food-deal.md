title: What to expect on a food deal night
faqs:
  - q: Is the deal at every stand?
    a: Usually only at designated stands. The team's listing or in-stadium signage names them.
  - q: Is there a limit per person?
    a: Most deals cap at a few items per transaction.
---
Food deal nights are concession promotions, most often dollar or discounted hot dogs, sodas or beer, sometimes a kids-eat-free offer with a ticket. The deal is usually limited to specific stands, a set number per transaction and sometimes a time window such as before first pitch or through a certain inning or period.

Lines at the participating stands are the longest part of the night. If the deal is the point of your visit, head to the stand before the game starts. Deals rarely require a special ticket, but some are tied to a ticket package or a specific section.
