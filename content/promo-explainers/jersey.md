title: What to expect at a jersey giveaway
faqs:
  - q: Can I pick my size?
    a: Rarely. Most jersey giveaways are one-size or distributed in limited sizes while they last.
  - q: Is it a real jersey?
    a: Usually a replica or fashion jersey, not an on-field authentic.
  - q: Is there an age limit?
    a: Adult jersey nights are often open to all fans. Kids jersey nights usually cap at 14 and under. Check the listing.
---
Jersey giveaways are replica or fashion jerseys handed out at the gates, one per fan, in a fixed quantity. They are typically one-size or offered in a small range of adult sizes, and sizes are not guaranteed. Many teams run a separate kids jersey night with youth sizes and an age limit.

Because the quantity is capped, arrive at gate opening on a high-demand night. Some jersey nights are tied to a theme ticket rather than offered to all fans, especially when the jersey is a special design such as a city, heritage or throwback edition. Read the team's listing for the age and ticket requirements before you buy.
