title: What to expect on a Hispanic heritage night
faqs:
  - q: Do I need a special ticket?
    a: For the game, no. For the themed item, usually yes, as part of a theme ticket package.
  - q: When are heritage nights?
    a: Most fall between mid-September and mid-October, though some teams schedule them earlier.
---
Hispanic and Latino heritage nights usually run during Hispanic Heritage Month in September and October, with some teams scheduling them earlier in the season. Expect pregame music and dance performances, local community groups recognized on the field or ice and food specials at select stands. Many teams release a special jersey or shirt with heritage-inspired design or a Spanish-language wordmark.

The themed item is usually included with a heritage theme ticket package rather than handed out at the gates. Some teams pair the night with a gate giveaway as well. The team's listing says which, and whether a portion of proceeds goes to a community partner.
