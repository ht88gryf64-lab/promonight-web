title: What to expect on a Pride night
faqs:
  - q: Do I get the Pride jersey with any ticket?
    a: Usually only with the Pride theme ticket package.
  - q: Where does the money go?
    a: Many teams donate a portion of theme-ticket proceeds to a local LGBTQ+ nonprofit. The team's listing names the partner.
---
Pride nights celebrate LGBTQ+ fans and communities. Most include a themed ticket package with a rainbow-accented shirt, hat or jersey, a pregame event on the plaza or concourse and a portion of theme-ticket proceeds donated to a local LGBTQ+ organization. In-game elements usually include a ceremonial first pitch or puck drop, video features and recognition of community partners.

The themed item is almost always part of the theme ticket, not a gate giveaway, so a regular ticket gets you the game and the atmosphere but not the item. Theme packages sell out ahead of time on many teams.
