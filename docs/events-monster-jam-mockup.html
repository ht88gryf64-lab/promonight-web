Going with kids

Does my toddler need a ticket? At most venues, children under 2 get in free on a lap, and ages 2 and up need a ticket. Some venues ticket every guest, so check before you buy.
Is it loud? Yes. Ear protection is sold at the event while supplies last, and you can bring your own from home.
