Hi, I'm Matt. I'm a Minnesota sports fan across the board: Twins, Vikings, Wild, Wolves, Lynx, Loons and Gophers. PromoNight started with the Twins, when I was trying to figure out which game to take my son to and realized there was no decent way to answer that question. Every team buries its promo schedule in a different corner of its website.

What started as a list for myself now tracks every giveaway, theme night, food deal and kids event across 169 teams in seven leagues. If you have ever shown up to a game and found out you missed bobblehead night by a day, this is for you.

FAQ

Questions? We've Got Answers.

What is PromoNight?

PromoNight tracks every promotional event at professional sports games across MLB, NBA, NFL, NHL, MLS, WNBA and college football. Giveaways, theme nights, food deals and kids events for 169 teams, in one calendar.

Where does the promo data come from?

Directly from official team sources: team websites, ticketing platforms and press releases. Every promo is checked against the source page before it appears here.

How often is it updated?

MLB schedules are rechecked weekly year-round. WNBA and MLS are rechecked weekly in season. Other leagues are updated as new announcements are confirmed.

Is PromoNight free?

Yes. Browsing every team and every promo is free. PromoNight Pro adds push notifications the morning of each promo for the teams you follow.

Which leagues are covered?

MLB, NBA, NFL, NHL, MLS, WNBA and college football, across 169 professional teams and 87 FBS schools.
