### 4. Defects

**D1. The two-line clamp on listing titles is dead; titles run to six lines at 390 and the tiles are ragged. 390, 768, 1280; branch only (the block does not exist on production).** `390-branch.png` at 766 to 993 px; `768-branch.png` at 691 to 953 px. The title span carries `line-clamp-2 block`. In the served stylesheet `.line-clamp-2` (`display:-webkit-box; -webkit-box-orient:vertical; -webkit-line-clamp:2; overflow:hidden`) is emitted **before** `.block` (`display:block`): dev CSS lines 920 and 932, production's minified CSS at byte offsets 21,501 and 21,628. `display:block` wins, and `-webkit-line-clamp` does nothing without `-webkit-box`. Computed on the branch: `display: block`, `-webkit-line-clamp: 2`, `overflow: hidden`, not clamped. Measured title heights: 97 / 81 / 81 px at 390 (6, 5 and 5 lines of 16.2 px), 49 px (3 lines) at 768 and at 1280. Setting `display:-webkit-box` on the span in the live page drops each title to 32.4 px (two lines), which is what the class promised. Consequences: the anchors are 227 / 211 / 211 px tall at 390 so the row's bottom edge steps; the `title` attribute tooltip, which exists to reveal a truncated title, duplicates text that is never truncated; and the block is 65 px taller than designed at 390. Severity: medium; introduced by this branch.

**D2. The third listing is not this bobblehead. All viewports; branch only.** `390-branch.png`, third tile at 766 px: "2012-13 Julius Erving Bobblehead Philadelphia 76ers Toyota Mini Series SGA NIB", $23.98, under the lead "Current listings for this bobblehead." It is a fourteen-year-old 76ers giveaway. The lead makes a claim the data does not meet. The stub holds four listings read from eBay's own search for this promo, so the query itself returns off-target items and the guard that kept three let this one through; the live Browse API will behave the same way. The first tile ("PRESALE 2026 Dr. J Julius Erving Brooklyn Nets SGA Bobblehead 11/18", $65.00) is this item, sold before anyone has one; the lead does not say these are pre-sales. Severity: medium (content accuracy); introduced by this branch.

**D3. The commerce block outranks the game. 390 and 1280 especially; branch only.** `390-branch.png`: the block begins at 683 px, 0.81 screens down, so its heading (698 px) and the tops of the three tiles are on the first screen; at 1280x800 the heading is at 585 px, also above the fold; at 768 the heading is at 623 px. After a two-sentence About, the next thing a fan who has not yet been to the game reads is three resale prices, and because Raptive's first content container now follows the block, THIS GAME (opponent, "also at this game: ABA Night", the home-game timeline) drops from 683 to 1,351 px, 1.6 screens down at 390. The fan guide's own sentence "Bobbleheads trade actively on eBay within hours of the game, which is why the same figure can cost more a week later than the ticket did" now sits a full screen below a block that shows the figure for sale before the game. The spec places the block under About, so this is a placement ruling, not a slip; I report it because the page as rendered puts what the bobblehead costs on eBay ahead of who the Nets are playing. Severity: medium (judgement).

**D4. The thumbnails are letterboxed slivers. All viewports; branch only.** `390-branch.png` at 766 px; `1280-branch.png` at 653 px. eBay's `s-l400` thumbnails are not square: natural sizes 146x400, 231x400 and 400x300. `object-contain` on `#ebe4d6` therefore shows the first listing as a 38 px-wide strip in a 103 px tile at 390 (37% of the tile; 68 of 186 px at 768; 84 of 229 px at 1280), the second as a 60 px strip, and the third with beige bands above and below. The tiles read as beige squares with a sliver of product; the price is the strongest visual in the block. Severity: low.

**D5. A screen reader hears each listing title twice. All viewports; branch only.** The anchor's accessible name is the image alt (the full listing title, 78 to 80 characters), then the price, then the visible title span (the same text), and the anchor's `title` attribute repeats it a third time as a tooltip. **D6. The listing destination could not be shown, and the campaign id is the dev value. Branch only.** Tapping tile 1 opened a new tab to `https://www.ebay.com/itm/227573531786?mkcid=1&mkrid=711-53200-19255-0&siteid=0&campid=5339999999&customid=web_promo_page_brooklyn_nets_2026_11_18_Julius_Erving_Bobblehead&toolid=10001&mkevt=1`, host `www.ebay.com`, **zero redirect hops**. eBay answered **403** with its "Error Page | eBay" ("SORRY Something went wrong on our end") to headless Chromium, to headless Google Chrome (the `chrome` channel) and to curl. Severity: low.
