## 1. Baseline differences (branch vs production)

Expected: none, since both carry cef28bd. Found: one HTML difference, caused by an environment variable; zero content differences in pixels once ad offsets are aligned; the rest of the deltas are Raptive runtime behaviour.

### 1a. Served HTML of `<article>`

Inner HTML extracted from both documents (600 normalized lines each; deploy meta, `/_next/static` hashes, `?v=` strings, `nonce` attributes and `<script>` bodies stripped). **Not identical: one differing region.**

| Region | Branch | Production |
|---|---|---|
| "Also on Ticketmaster" anchor (`aria-label="Minnesota Wild tickets, also on Ticketmaster"`) | `href="https://www.ticketmaster.com/minnesota-wild-tickets/artist/805974"` | `href="https://ticketmaster.evyy.net/c/7236189/264167/4272?u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.ticketmaster.com%2Fminnesota-wild-tickets%2Fartist%2F805974&sharedid=web_promo_page_minnesota-wild&subId3=blaze-orange-hat"` |

Cause: `NEXT_PUBLIC_TICKETMASTER_IMPACT_WRAP` is not defined in `.env.local` (only in `.env.example`), so `src/lib/affiliates.ts:19` falls back to the bare Ticketmaster URL on the dev server. This is an environment difference, not a code difference; the "Get tickets" TicketNetwork href is byte-identical in both. Everything else in the article, including all 18 `/promos/` anchors, the rail cards, the FAQ and the provenance strings, is identical.

Note: the Raptive ad containers (`div.adthrive-ad ...`) are not present in the served HTML of either environment and no element other than the `<article>` itself carries a `data-ad-region` or `data-ad-item` attribute. The containers are injected by the Raptive script at runtime, and that script ran on the dev server too (body receives `adthrive-device-phone`; six unfilled containers at 390 versus five filled on production).

## 3. Defects

**D1. Browser back from a rail card lands one full screen below where the fan left. 390. Reproduces on production.** `2026-11-08-main/blaze-orange-hat/390-branch-rail-before-click.png` (before) and `390-branch-after-back.png` (after).
Flow: scroll so MORE MINNESOTA WILD GIVEAWAYS is at the top (scrollY 2,724), tap the first card, browser back. The browser restores 2,724 (smooth-scrolled over ~0.7 s), holds it for 1-3 s, then the window jumps to 3,764: **+1,040 px, fails the 50 px bound, 3 of 3 branch runs and 1 of 1 production run.** At 3,764 the fan sees the last FAQ row ("Do I need a special ticket?"), the commission disclosure, a blank ad container and the top of the footer; the rail is 1,040 px above. Mechanism, measured: no JavaScript scroll call fires (scrollIntoView, scrollTo, scrollBy, focus were all hooked; none recorded after back). A MutationObserver shows Raptive removing `div.adthrive-ad.adthrive-footer` and `div.adthrive-sticky-outstream` from `<body>` and re-adding them plus `div.adthrive-ad.adthrive-below-post` inside the article about 1.5 s after back; the jump is logged at that exact mutation. With `overflow-anchor: none` injected, the same flow holds at 2,724 (0 px delta), so this is Chromium scroll anchoring reacting to the ad teardown and re-insertion. The forward tap into the card is correct (lands at scrollY 0).

**D2. The season timeline dots are real buttons 5.5x5.5 px. All viewports; 390 shown.** `390-branch.png` at y 1,144 (inside THIS GAME). Six `<g role="button" tabindex="0" aria-pressed>` elements, each a circle of r=5 (r=6 for the current game) in a 600x40 viewBox stretched to the card width: 5.5 px on a side at 390, about 10-12 px at 768 and 1280. They work: tapping "Nov 25" flips `aria-pressed` to true, grows the dot and changes the caption to "Nov 25 · Native American Heritage Night Hat · home game 9 of 42" without navigating. Nothing on the surface tells a fan the dots are tappable, and a thumb cannot hit a 5 px target. The group's aria-label ("Minnesota Wild home schedule, 42 games, 6 promos") has no visible counterpart.

**D3. 47 of 61 interactive elements at 390 are under 44x44.** `390-branch.png`. Only 14 meet the size: Get tickets (144x52), Share, Parking, Hotels, Remind me, the rail cards and the NHL rail rows. Under size, by position: "Open menu" button 40x40; breadcrumb "NHL" 24x18, "Minnesota Wild" 82x18, "Promos" 42x18; "Grand Casino Arena" venue link 135x16 (the only above-the-fold path to the arena page); "Also on Ticketmaster" 133x21; image-share button 40x40; figcaption "source" 33x12; "nhl.com" 43x14; "New York Islanders" 128x16; six timeline dots 5.5x5.5 (D2); "grandcasinoarena.com ›" 123x12 (twice); "Grand Casino Arena gameday guide" 328x21; "All Minnesota Wild promos" 168x21; "Jersey and apparel giveaways across pro sports" 302x21; the three FAQ `summary` rows 328x21 (the whole row is the only way to open an answer); "Get the week's giveaways in your inbox →" 266x20; 22 footer links 15 px tall; "Do not sell or share my personal information." 259x15; "Terms of Content Use" 126x15. Counts at 768 and 1280: 52 and 53 of 67.

**D4. Brand red on cream fails 4.5:1 everywhere it is used as text: 4.33:1.** All viewports, both environments. `390-branch.png`. rgb(218,45,32) on rgb(247,243,234): "NIGHT" in the wordmark (18 px/800), "Grand Casino Arena" (15 px), "Also on Ticketmaster" (14 px), "nhl.com" (12 px), "All Minnesota Wild promos" and "Jersey and apparel giveaways across pro sports" (14 px). axe `color-contrast` (serious) reports 11 nodes. Related axe `link-in-text-block` (serious, 2 nodes): the figcaption "source" link and the "nhl.com" link are distinguished from surrounding text by this colour alone, no underline.

**D5. THIS GAME restates the header.** 390, `390-branch.png` y 954-1,203. "Thursday, Oct 29, 2026 · 7:00 PM" appears at 268 px in the header and again at 1,038 px as GAME; the opponent appears as "Minnesota Wild vs Islanders" (header) and "OPPONENT New York Islanders" (card); "Home game 5 of 42" is a row and is repeated in the caption directly beneath the timeline ("Oct 29 · Blaze Orange Hat · home game 5 of 42"). The 249 px card adds one fact a fan did not already have.

**D6. Six blank grey boxes in the branch render at 390** (1,213, 1,901, 2,450, 3,390, 3,719, 3,901 px; 100-250 px tall), `390-branch.png`. These are Raptive containers injected after load and left unfilled on the dev server. On production they fill (300, 300, 250, 250, 250 px), moving everything below each one down by 50-250 px between 1.2 s and 3.4 s (CLS 0.0113 at 390, within budget but all of it from this). The page reserves no height in its own markup, so what a fan sees below the first ad depends on what Raptive inserts.

**D7. Production only, 390: Raptive's sticky outstream video and anchor ad cover the bottom 183 px of the first viewport**, including the hat image (`390-production.png`, top; iframes at 661-844 px: a 178x100 player and a 390x183 container, plus the "gg-ad-iframe" overlay). Its controls are "Close sticky video" 22x20, "Close advertisement" 24x22 and a "×" at 2.12:1. Third-party, but it is what a first-time visitor on a phone sees.

**D8. Jargon.** "Source read Oct 7, 2026" and "read Nov 6, 2026" (three times) are provenance language a fan has no frame for; "GENERAL GUIDE" is an internal section class shown as an eyebrow label. The breadcrumb's last crumb "Promos" is a link to the team page anchor, so the current page has no crumb.

Not a defect: the black "N" button at bottom-left of every branch screenshot is the Next.js dev-tools indicator and does not exist on production.

## 5. One recommendation

Fix D1 before anything else on this page and verify it on production, not on the dev server: a fan who taps any rail card and comes back loses their place by a full screen, every time, today. The measurement that proves the fix is the one used here: rail heading at the top (scrollY 2,724), tap a card, back, wait five seconds, scrollY within 50 px of 2,724, with the Raptive script running. The same test should then be added to the live battery, because the trigger is the ad script's teardown and re-insertion after back, which no served-HTML check can see.
